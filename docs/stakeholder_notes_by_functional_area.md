# Stakeholder Interview Notes Organised by Functional Area and Theme

## Finance

### Finance Analyst

#### Delayed or Missed Follow-up
- We have cases sitting in 'awaiting callback' status for months because the promised date was never recorded.
- Cases pile up on Fridays because no one wants to open new ones before the weekend.

#### Poor Account-Status Visibility
- We have never measured how often a case comes back because we have no way to track it.
- Status updates do not capture why a case changed state, only that it did.
- No one has visibility of what is actually in the pipeline or how long cases typically take.

#### Lack of Confidence in the Numbers
- Reporting takes so long that by the time we see the numbers, they are already out of date.
- The finance team cannot forecast recovery revenue because they do not trust the activity data.

#### Change Resistance or Distrust
- Some representatives have been doing this for so long they are faster than the system, and they will resist change.

#### Miscellaneous
- We lose customers because the recovery process is so slow they lose faith we will ever resolve it.
- If self-service reduces representative workload, that headcount saving needs to be real, not theoretical.
- We have edge cases involving hardship, vulnerability, and regulatory forbearance that need human judgment.

---

### Finance Business Partner / Finance & Compliance Director

#### Duplicated Work
- Every status update in the old system requires manual re-keying into the spreadsheet.

#### Customer Friction
- Customers call back three times because they do not remember what they were told on the first call.

#### Miscellaneous
- The current system treats payment promises the same as payment confirmations, which creates confusion.

---

## Compliance

### Compliance Liaison

#### Duplicated Work
- Every new tool we add makes the job harder because it adds another place where data can get out of sync.

#### Delayed or Missed Follow-up
- We could recover an extra 5% of balances if we just contacted people at the right time.

#### Poor Account-Status Visibility
- The audit trail is scattered across email, spreadsheets, and the legacy database, making compliance reviews a nightmare.
- A case marked 'resolved' in one system might be 'in progress' in another.
- We do not have a way to identify which cases are straightforward versus which require specialist handling.
- Audit trails across multiple systems make it nearly impossible to demonstrate what happened to a case.

#### Customer Friction
- Customers respond well when they have control and transparency about what they owe.

#### Lack of Confidence in the Numbers
- We are losing money on cases that fall through the cracks between systems.

#### Change Resistance or Distrust
- We need to measure whether this is better before we commit to rolling it out.
- Representatives are afraid to try new things because they were burned by the last system update.

#### Miscellaneous
- The database was designed for a tenth of the volume we handle now, and performance degrades as we approach month-end.
- The scale of the problem means that even small improvements in efficiency multiply significantly.
- The 20-year-old system was built for a different business model entirely.
- The details matter more than the vision here.
- We lose good people because they get frustrated managing around the system instead of managing cases.
- Representatives see solutions that the system does not allow them to implement.
- The best representatives are being held back by process rather than enabled by it.
- If the portal works, it will work because it gives customers control, not because it is fancier.
- The portal could work brilliantly for the 40% of customers who want to resolve this themselves.
- The portal would be great, but what happens when someone disputes the balance?

## Collections & Customer Service

### Collections Representative

#### Duplicated Work
- The collections database does not sync with the email tracker, so representatives often re-contact customers who were already promised callbacks.
- Most of our experienced representatives have learned workarounds that make them faster than any official process.
- We are paying representatives to hunt for information that should already be on the screen.
- Experienced representatives are faster because they have memorised workarounds that should not be necessary.
- No one can tell me how much the current workarounds are costing us because the cost is hidden.
- We lose at least 20% of follow-ups because they fall between shifts and no one owns the handoff.

#### Delayed or Missed Follow-up
- Customers go through the contact process multiple times because we have no way to prevent re-contact.
- We reach out to customers in a random order rather than any strategic sequence.

#### Poor Account-Status Visibility
- The data quality is so poor that we stopped running management reports altogether.
- The activity log does not distinguish between actual contact and failed contact attempts.
- There is no standard definition of what each status actually means across the team.

#### Customer Friction
- We are exposed to complaint risk because customers cannot track what we have told them.

#### Change Resistance or Distrust
- Representatives are afraid of the portal because they think it will take away their job, not because it won't work.
- The compliance team says they cannot approve anything until they see the future process mapped out.

#### Miscellaneous
- The old system was designed for half the volume we handle now, and it shows.
- New representatives take two weeks longer to reach productivity because they have to learn the spreadsheet system.
- Representatives are ready for better tools; they are tired of workarounds.
- If we can reduce the time between identifying a payment opportunity and making contact, we win.
- Self-service works well for straightforward cases, but we need to be honest about which ones those are.
- I can see that work is happening, but I cannot see whether it is the right work.
- If unsupported cases come back with no context, representatives will reject the portal.
- Every month the finance team has to reconcile our activity count with the database, and they never match.
- Leadership approved the roadmap five years ago and never came back to debt recovery.
