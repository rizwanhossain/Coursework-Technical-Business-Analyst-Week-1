# JTBD and Prioritisation

## JTBD statements

- When I manage my workload, I want straightforward cases to be prioritized appropriately so they're resolved quickly.

- When I prepare to contact a customer, I want to see how many times they've been contacted this month so I don't duplicate outreach.

- When I review a case's history, I want to understand why it changed status so I can make informed decisions.

- When I record customer communication about payment, I want to distinguish between payment promises and confirmed payments so there's no confusion about what's actually owed.

- When I schedule a customer callback, I want the system to track and enforce the deadline so callbacks happen on time.

- When I report on case activity, I want consistent status definitions across the team so reporting is accurate and actionable.

## Prioritisation justification

1. When I schedule a customer callback, I want the system to track and enforce the deadline so callbacks happen on time.

This job takes the top priority spot as its current state leads to loss of revenue, poor chains of customer communication, and severe instances of delinquency going under the radar. It would take the burden off the representative and give them more time to do meaningful work, rather than monitoring when follow-ups are needed.

2. When I record customer communication about payment, I want to distinguish between payment promises and confirmed payments so there's no confusion about what's actually owed.

This job would streamline the customer communication and collections process. Customers and representatives would be clear about what's owed and by when. Time spent clarifying details in future calls would be minimised. It would save representatives time chasing resolved cases.

3. When I prepare to contact a customer, I want to see how many times they've been contacted this month so I don't duplicate outreach.

This would free up representatives and allow the to reassign their efforts to customers who haven't been contacted yet. It would also prevent breaching contact laws. Less harassment would increase customer satisfaction, which may increase cooperation when repaying delinquent balances.
