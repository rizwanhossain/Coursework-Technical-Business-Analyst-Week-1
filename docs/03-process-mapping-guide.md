# Process-Mapping

## Current-state Summary
For the customer, they are unsure of their previous interactions and agreements with the bank. They call in for simple enquires, such as current balance, repayment structure, and money owed. They are contacted multiple times about the same enquiries, often passed between representatives who ask for the same details each time.
For representatives, the process is arduous. They have to cross-reference different sources of truth and make notes and reminders manually. There is no consistency is logging procedure so status codes assigned to accounts are practically meaningless. They waste time following up with accounts who have already been contacted and they have no way of distinguishing between simple and complex cases.
For managers and team leaders, they are seeing a lack of optimisation, wasted efforts, and lost revenue from the processes their teams are following. When cases are escalated, they aren't provided with sufficient details and have to begin building a legible case file from scratch.

## Self-service and Automation Opportunities
- Customers should be able to check their own balances.
- Payments should be able to be made independently through the portal.
- Representatives should be automatically reminded about follow-ups.
- Customers should be able to view records of their previous conversations/agreements, instead of calling the bank to clarify.
- Records of customer communication should be logged automatically in a centralised system.
